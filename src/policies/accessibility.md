## Our goal

We aim for this site to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at level AA.

## What we have done

- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
- Text and background colours meet WCAG AA contrast ratios.
- Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels, so screen readers can navigate them.
- Text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content, and the layout adapts to small screens.
- Reports use a readable line length and type size.
- Images that carry meaning have text alternatives.
- The site works without JavaScript, except for search, which offers a link to browse all research instead.

## Known limitations

- **PDF downloads** may not be fully accessible to every screen reader. Every report is also available as an accessible web page on this site.
- **Older reports** or third-party figures may lack detailed text descriptions. We fix these when we find them.

## Alternative formats

If you need a report in another format, such as large print, plain text or an accessible Word document, [contact us](/contact/) and we will do our best to provide it.

## Feedback

If you find something on the site that is hard to use, please tell us which page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the [Australian Human Rights Commission](https://humanrights.gov.au/complaints).
