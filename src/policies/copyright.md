## How to credit us

When you reuse a report, you must:

1. name the authors and {{ site.name }};
2. give the report's title and a link to it;
3. link to the licence;
4. say if you made changes.

**Example:**

> Based on "Fair wages for migrant workers: what the published evidence says" by the {{ site.name }} Research Team, {{ site.name }}, 2026, {{ site.url }}/reports/…, licensed under {{ site.legal.contentLicence.short }}. Translated into Spanish.

The "Cite" button on each report gives you a ready-made citation.

## Copyright concerns

We respect the rights of others. If you believe material on this site infringes your copyright, email us with:

- the material and the page it appears on;
- the work you believe is infringed;
- your contact details and a statement that you own, or are authorised to act for the owner of, the copyright.

We will review your request promptly and, where appropriate, remove or correct the material.
