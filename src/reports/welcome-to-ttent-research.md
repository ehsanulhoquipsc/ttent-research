## Why this site exists

tTENT is a non-profit research workspace. Research groups use it to plan their work, divide it up and write their findings. This site is the public side: every finished, reviewed report is published here for anyone to read.

## What you will find

Each report has a short description, a contents list, the full text to read online and, where available, a PDF to download. Reports are grouped into topics, and you can search every report from the search page.

## Reading in Bangla

Where a report has a Bangla edition, a language switch appears at the top of the report so you can move between English and বাংলা.

## Get involved

If you have a question worth researching, or want to join a project as a researcher, translator or reviewer, [send us a message](/contact/).
